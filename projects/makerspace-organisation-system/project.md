title: 'Makerspace Organisation System'
description: >-
  A collection of small 3D-printed projects developed to improve organisation
  and day-to-day use of the makerspace.

  Rather than relying on generic storage solutions, I designed and printed a
  range of purpose-built holders, organisers and mounting solutions for the
  tools, components and equipment used around the workspace. The projects
  include modular storage for smaller components, dedicated holders for tools
  and equipment, and accessories that integrate with the existing organisation
  system.

  The emphasis was on making practical solutions for the actual workspace:
  keeping frequently used items accessible, making small components easier to
  find, and using otherwise unused wall and workstation space more effectively.

  The result is less a single product than an evolving collection of small
  fabrication projects. Each piece addresses a specific organisational
  problem, while the system as a whole makes the makerspace easier to work in
  and adapt as its needs change.

  The photographs show the system in use, individual storage and mounting
  solutions, and parts of the fabrication process.
